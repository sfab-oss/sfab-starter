# ALW-715 Demote comment-hygiene out of AGENTS.md (template soft policy hurts)

## Context

ALW-526 added comment-hygiene docs plus standing bullets in `AGENTS.md` / `.claude/CLAUDE.md`. On a **template** repo those front-door rules hurt more than they help: agents over-delete teaching comments (races, money ordering, RBAC), bike-shed comment PRs, and soft policy with no lint gate is the worst middle. Platform now has a different tagged-comment gate (ALW-688) — keep starter standards from competing.

## Scope

- Remove the four comment-hygiene bullets from `AGENTS.md` and mirror into `.claude/CLAUDE.md` (byte-identical).
- Soften `docs/guides/comment-hygiene.md` with a short banner: optional human reference for scrubs; **not** standing agent policy; do not strip useful teaching comments in this template.
- Do **not** port platform `check-comment-tags` lint.
